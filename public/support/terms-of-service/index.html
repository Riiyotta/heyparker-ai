21. Save to Parker Browser Extension

This section governs your access to and use of the "Save to Parker" Chrome browser extension (the "Extension"), a complementary distribution of Parker that you install from the Chrome Web Store. By installing or using the Extension, you agree to these Terms in their entirety, including this section.

21.1 Description of the Extension

The Extension is a single-purpose tool that lets you save advertisements and social media posts you encounter while browsing supported platforms to your Parker visual ideas board. The supported surfaces, enumerated in our Privacy Policy §16.1, are: the Facebook Ad Library; organic Facebook posts and photos on facebook.com (post permalinks, photo pages, the home feed, and profile and Page timelines — excluding Groups, Events, Marketplace, and Stories); the Facebook Reels viewer and reels in the home feed; Facebook video posts (watch pages and profile or Page video pages); Instagram posts, reels, carousels, and Stories (the home feed, post and reel permalinks, profile and Explore grids, the Reels viewer, and the Stories viewer — excluding Story Highlights and Direct messages); organic LinkedIn posts containing images or video (the home feed, post permalinks including shareable linkedin.com/posts/… links, and company and member activity tabs — excluding messaging, jobs, notifications, My Network, settings, text-only posts, document posts, and the public LinkedIn Ad Library); Pinterest pin detail pages and pin cards on Pinterest feed, board, profile, and search pages (across all country subdomains); TikTok video and photo detail pages, together with the TikTok browsing surfaces where the Save button can be attached to the videos and photo posts you open (your feed, Explore, Search results, hashtag pages, sound pages, account profile grids including the Reposts tab, and playlists); and YouTube Shorts. The Extension adds a "Save to Parker" button on these surfaces; clicking the button opens a dialog where you select an organization, brand, and board, and triggers a single save. The Extension does not perform any function unrelated to this single purpose.

21.2 Acceptable Use of the Extension

In addition to the general acceptable-use restrictions in §8, when using the Extension you agree:

21.5 Browser Permissions

The Extension requests a limited set of Chrome browser permissions strictly necessary to provide the save-to-Parker feature:

  • Host access to supported platforms (facebook.com, instagram.com, tiktok.com, youtube.com, *.pinterest.com) — required to inject the Save button and to read the content you are saving.

  • Host access to Parker's backend (app.heyparker.ai, auth.heyparker.ai) — required to send saved ideas and to sign you in.

  • storage — to persist your Parker authentication tokens and last-used save preferences locally, and to cache a small set of non-identifying technical identifiers (Facebook GraphQL query IDs) used to keep the supported-platform integrations working.
